About

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at KAIST, advised by Prof. Tae-Hyun Oh (AMI Lab). I study how multimodal LLMs and agents encode and reason over abstract visual representations — vector graphics, GUIs, and symbols such as emoji and gestures — beyond natural images. My recent work covers interactive GUI grounding (EMNLP 2026), speech–vision hallucination (CVPR 2026), training-free SVG–image retrieval (WACV 2026), and cultural preference in LLM/VLM agents.

Before my Ph.D., I was an AI Research Scientist at POSTECH, an AI Research Engineer at the LG Electronics CTO AI Lab, and an AI education manager at Upstage. I also co-organize AGI Korea (formerly TensorFlow Korea), an AI community of 66K+ members.
